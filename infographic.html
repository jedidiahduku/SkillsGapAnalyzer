Skills Gap Analyzer

A free career-guidance tool for people locked out of it. Match the skills you have to your best-fit roles — or search a job you want and see exactly which skills it needs.

01 · The Problem
40%
of workers in developing economies hit by skills mismatch

Talent is universal. Access to guidance isn't.

People make upskilling and career decisions on word-of-mouth and guesswork — not data. They spend months learning the wrong skills, locked out of roles they could already qualify for.

— International Labour Organization (ILO)
